My Name

(Short and funny story about my name)

While my last name is pronounced "Reich", it is written "Raij" as my family immigrated from Uruguay and there "J" sound like "ch". Funny story is that my first name is also affected by this immigration story, since my parents looked for a simple name that will include only easy to pronounce letters!
